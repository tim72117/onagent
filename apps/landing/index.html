Why onagent

Everything you need to wire AI into your UI

From LLM infrastructure to AI that drives your UI, to serving real customers — all in one place.

No LLM infrastructure to build

No picking a model, wiring up an API, or handling retries and timeouts yourself — onagent takes care of the LLM inference infrastructure so you can just define tools.

AI drives your UI directly

Tool calls dispatch straight to the handler you registered: fill forms, navigate, check boxes, click buttons — not just a reply, but a real action.

Wires straight into your existing UI

No frontend rewrite needed. Map tools to what's already there, and AI can operate it — works with a pure frontend, no backend changes required.
